A **subscription** is a URL your provider hosts.
VX fetches it periodically and turns the content into nodes you can connect to.
This tutorial is for users that do not know how to import subscription into VX.

UI labels in the screenshots are in Chinese; English equivalents are noted below.

Follow these steps if you prefer text and screenshots.

### 1. Copy the subscription link from your provider

Open your provider’s dashboard. Under **Subscription** (订阅), click **Subscription link** (订阅链接), then choose **Copy** (复制).

![Copy the subscription link from the provider dashboard](/tutorials/import-subscription/1.png)

### 2. Add the subscription in VX

In VX, open **Nodes** (节点), switch to the **Subscription** (订阅) tab, tap **+**, then choose **Clipboard** (剪贴板).

![Add a subscription from the clipboard](/tutorials/import-subscription/2.png)

### 3. Set a remark (optional)

When prompted **Add a remark?** (添加一个备注？), enter a name for the subscription, then tap **OK** (确定). Tap **Skip** (跳过) to keep the default name.

![Set an optional remark for the subscription](/tutorials/import-subscription/3.png)

### 4. Check the imported nodes

Switch to the **Nodes** (节点) tab. Your new nodes appear in the list (you can filter by the subscription remark).

![Imported nodes appear in the node list](/tutorials/import-subscription/4.png)
