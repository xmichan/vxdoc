A **subscription** is a URL your provider hosts. 
VX fetches it periodically and turns the content into nodes you can connect to. 
This tutorial is for users that do not know how to import subscription into VX.

Watch the walkthrough below, or open it on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rpGnbPtFMXI).
